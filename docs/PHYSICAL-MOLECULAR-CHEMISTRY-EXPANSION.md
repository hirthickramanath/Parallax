# Physical and molecular chemistry expansion

CH1.1–CH1.13 and CH2.1–CH2.6 now have 38 added numeric transfer questions, 19 model-assumption checks, derivation extensions and 19 embedded analytic investigations. Original theory is retained. Coverage includes chemical potential and standard states, phase/rate models, activated barriers, molecular state counting, catalysis, activities, colligative laws, equilibrium/electrode response, diffusion, photochemistry, molecular orbitals, avoided crossings, variational methods and spectroscopy.

Model assumptions are explicit. Activity and reaction quotients are dimensionless; electron and quantum counts remain integer where required. A normalized rate display is not a universal microscopic mechanism, a two-state crossing does not integrate nuclear dynamics, a Gaussian benchmark is not a completed molecular calculation, and an angular Larmor clock is not a complete NMR spectrum.

Validation: production build passed; all 420 unit/content/model/evidence tests passed; all three affected browser cases and all 104 full browser cases passed. Checks include derivative slopes at electrode equilibrium, coverage saturation, variational lower-bound agreement in the exact oscillator benchmark, integer rotational spacings and intensity sign invariance. Browser authentication is mocked; this release does not establish live cloud behavior.

Eight new partial mappings bring the ledger to 124. Exact OpenStax Chemistry source elements and hashes cover reaction Gibbs/quotient relations, ideal Raoult pressure, first-order integration, activation-versus-reaction energy, catalytic pathways, freezing-point limiting behavior, Nernst voltage and MO bond counting. Advanced extensions outside those scopes remain internally checked, not source-verified in full. All 354 lessons remain drafts, with zero independent approvals.

At this checkpoint 179 lessons lack explicit widgets (implicit models excluded). Review packets are generated for every lesson and bind model-source dependencies as well as lesson text. Genuine independent scientific approval, learner evaluation and real project execution remain distinct work.
