<LessonSection id="concept" title="Definitions and hypotheses" layer="concept">

Inflation is accelerated expansion, ä>0, often modeled by a field whose energy density and pressure can produce a shrinking comoving Hubble scale.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derivation, proof and limitations" layer="theory" fullOnly>

For a canonical homogeneous scalar in flat FLRW, use c=ℏ=1 units: $\rho=\dot\phi^2/2+V$ and p=φ̇²/2−V. Potential dominance can give p≈−ρ and accelerated expansion through the Friedmann acceleration equation.

The field equation is φ̈+3Hφ̇+V′=0 in that minimal model. Slow roll neglects specified acceleration/kinetic terms only when its dimensionless smallness conditions hold. Not every potential supports enough duration or a viable exit, and noncanonical/multifield scenarios need different predictions.

The number of expansion e-folds is $N=\ln(a_{end}/a_{start})$. The mapping from a present observable scale to its inflationary epoch depends on reheating and subsequent expansion, so one universal exact e-fold number is unjustified. Quantum fluctuation predictions require a perturbation state and model assumptions.

Near scale-invariant adiabatic perturbations are compatible with broad inflationary classes but do not uniquely identify a potential. Tensor amplitudes, non-Gaussianity and isocurvature constrain classes conditionally. Reheating, initial conditions and the relation to a quantum-gravity description remain research questions, not experimentally settled details to fill with certainty.


### Derive, test and transfer

The number of e-folds is $N=\ln(a_f/a_i)=\int Hdt$. For approximately constant H, the scale factor grows exponentially and a fixed comoving length's proper size scales as eN. An increase in N is additive in logarithmic expansion but multiplicative in physical scale.

Inflationary model testing requires a mechanism, potential or effective dynamics, perturbation predictions and a reheating history. A simple exponential drawing does not solve initial-condition questions or establish a particular inflaton field. The display uses a small numerical range to make e-folding visible; realistic model durations must be justified in the full cosmic history.

</LessonSection>
