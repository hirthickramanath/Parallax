<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derivation, proof and limitations" layer="theory" fullOnly>

Take coarse-graining factor b>1 and linearized eigen-coupling u. If $u'=b^yu$, y>0 is relevant, y&lt;0 irrelevant and y=0 marginal at this linear order. Reversing the flow parameter convention can reverse displayed beta-function signs without changing the underlying physical classification.

Linearize a multi-coupling flow at a fixed point and find eigenvectors of its stability matrix. Individual original couplings need not already be scaling eigenfields. Nonlinear terms determine marginal behaviour and can generate couplings absent from a truncated ansatz.

A relevant tuning direction gives a correlation-length scaling ξ∝|t|⁻ν with ν=1/y_t in the simple scaling framework. Corrections to scaling, dangerous irrelevant variables and finite-size effects require qualifications. A computed fixed point of a truncation is not automatically an exact fixed point of the full theory.

RG applies to critical phenomena and quantum field theory with different physical scale interpretations. Regulators and renormalization schemes affect intermediate couplings; observable predictions must be compared with matched definitions. An asymptotic perturbative beta function requires a stated small-coupling/regime limit.


### Derivation and transfer

Near a fixed point, a linearized renormalization flow du/dℓ=y u gives u(ℓ)=u0 exp(yℓ). Positive y grows under increasing coarse-graining scale and is called relevant in this convention; negative y is irrelevant. A marginal y=0 needs nonlinear terms.

The display makes a chosen local eigenvalue explicit. It does not calculate beta functions from a microscopic theory or justify extrapolation far from the fixed point. Reversing flow conventions can reverse labels, so the direction of scale change must be stated.

</LessonSection>
