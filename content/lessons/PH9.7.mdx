<LessonSection id="story" title="Why the question matters" layer="story">

Density matrices describe incomplete access, statistical mixtures and environmental entanglement without assuming a fundamental collapse mechanism.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="concept" title="Definitions and hypotheses" layer="concept">

A density operator is positive with trace one. Reduced states arise by tracing inaccessible degrees of freedom from a joint state.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derivation, proof and limitations" layer="theory" fullOnly>

Outcome probabilities are $p(a)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P_a)$. A pure state obeys ρ²=ρ and Trρ²=1; mixed states have smaller purity in a finite-dimensional setting. A decomposition of one density matrix into pure states is not generally unique.

For system alternatives correlated with environment states e₀,e₁, the reduced off-diagonal coefficient is multiplied by ⟨e₁|e₀⟩. Orthogonal environment records suppress interference in that basis even when the joint state is pure. Global correlations remain; local decoherence is not the assertion that every interpretation’s measurement problem is solved.

A simple qubit dephasing model multiplies off-diagonal entries by e⁻γt while preserving populations. Trace, Hermiticity and positivity must be checked for any proposed map. Arbitrary element damping can violate positivity or fail complete positivity when extended to an ancilla.

Decoherence rates and preferred structures depend on the coupling and environment. Markovian master equations add approximations; memory and initial correlations can matter. Basis-specific coherence, purity, energy relaxation and entropy are different diagnostics.


### Derivation and transfer

A density matrix is positive semidefinite with unit trace. Purity Trρ² equals one for a pure state and is below one for a mixed state. For diagonal probabilities p and 1−p, purity is p²+(1−p)².

A diagonal state depends on basis; loss of off-diagonal entries in one representation does not by itself establish a physical decoherence mechanism. The display describes a stated diagonal mixture, not an environmental simulation or a state reconstructed from data.

</LessonSection>
