<LessonSection id="story" title="Why the question matters" layer="story">

Astrochemistry investigates formation and destruction in gas, dust surfaces and irradiated ices. Prebiotic relevance is a question about pathways and environments, not a conclusion that follows from the word organic.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="intuition" title="Build a mental model" layer="intuition">

A detected abundance is the balance of formation, destruction and transport. A plausible reaction on paper must also operate fast enough and dispose of excess energy in its actual environment.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="concept" title="Definitions and hypotheses" layer="concept">

An astrochemical network models species abundances through environment-dependent reaction rates and physical processes. Observational identification and pathway attribution are distinct inference tasks.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derivation, proof and limitations" layer="theory" fullOnly>

For species i with number density ni, a kinetic network has $dn_i/dt=\sum_r\nu_{ir}R_r$ plus any transport terms. A gas-phase two-body rate often has form R=k(T)nAnB in a stated regime, with k in volume per time. The coefficient is not a dimensionless equilibrium constant, and an overall balanced equation does not uniquely specify it.

Ion–molecule reactions, neutral reactions, photoprocesses and grain-surface processes have different constraints. At low density, a newly formed association complex may need photon emission or surface energy transfer to stabilize because a third-body collision is rare. Surface diffusion, binding energies, desorption and irradiation can control whether a route actually contributes.

A simplified abundance balance dx/dt=f−dx with constant formation rate f and first-order destruction coefficient d>0 approaches xeq=f/d with relaxation timescale 1/d. This illustrates that a steady abundance does not prove thermodynamic equilibrium. Changing radiation, density or grain conditions can invalidate a constant-rate steady-state approximation.

An astronomical molecular identification needs multiple consistent transitions where available, excitation and radiative-transfer modeling, velocity agreement and attention to spectral confusion and missing predicted lines. Laboratory frequencies and theoretical line lists have uncertainties and provenance. An apparently matching feature is evidence to evaluate, not an automatic detection.

Organic means carbon-based chemistry in the relevant convention, not biological production. A prebiotic precursor can be produced abiotically and still face major transport, concentration, stability and subsequent-reaction barriers before a biological pathway is relevant. Chiral excess, isotopic ratios or complex mixtures also need alternative-source and selection analyses. Keep abundance measurements, reaction-network hypotheses and origin claims at their different evidentiary levels.


### Derive, test and transfer

A simple dust-attenuated photodestruction coefficient is k=k0 exp(−γAV) for a specified effective extinction parameter. More shielding reduces that one loss channel, but molecular abundance also depends on formation, transport and other destruction reactions.

Laboratory formation of an organic building block under chosen conditions does not establish that those conditions occurred in every interstellar environment or that life arose. Radiation fields, temperature, surfaces and timescales must be matched. The display is a one-channel attenuation toy, not a solved prebiotic network or a biosignature detection.

</LessonSection>
