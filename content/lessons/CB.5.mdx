<LessonSection id="story" title="Why this matters" layer="story">

Organic molecules in interstellar clouds and meteorites are identified through structure-sensitive spectra. Counting atoms alone cannot distinguish connectivity or spatial arrangement, and detecting an organic molecule does not by itself demonstrate life.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="intuition" title="Build the picture" layer="intuition">

A molecular formula is an inventory, not a wiring diagram. A functional group is a local bonding pattern that suggests reactivity within a larger electronic and steric context.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="concept" title="State the model" layer="concept">

Organic structure requires connectivity and, when relevant, stereochemistry. Functional groups help organize properties but do not replace mechanisms or experimental evidence.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derive and qualify the result" layer="theory" fullOnly>

Carbon commonly forms four covalent bonds in neutral closed-shell structures, counted with bond order. Hydrocarbons include alkanes, alkenes, alkynes and aromatic systems. Functional groups include alcohol, ether, carbonyl, carboxylic acid, ester, amine and amide. An amide nitrogen differs from an amine because lone-pair delocalization changes basicity and geometry.

For a neutral conventional closed-shell formula containing C, H, N and monovalent halogens X, the degree of unsaturation is $(2C+2+N-H-X)/2$. Oxygen and divalent sulfur do not enter that count in the usual valence treatment. A ring or double bond contributes one and a triple bond two. The formula has assumptions and is not a universal rule for radicals, ions or unusual valences.

Constitutional isomers have different connectivity. Stereoisomers share connectivity but differ spatially. A tetrahedral centre with four distinct substituents is a common source of chirality, although a full molecular symmetry check is needed. Enantiomers are nonsuperimposable mirror images; a racemate can show no net optical rotation without its molecules becoming achiral.

Electron-pair movement organizes introductory mechanisms: nucleophiles donate an electron pair to an electrophilic centre. Curved arrows track pairs from their origin, not motion of a positive charge. Resonance contributors describe one delocalized state; they are not molecules rapidly interconverting. Substitution, addition, elimination and acid-base transfer are broad patterns whose pathways depend on substrate, solvent and conditions.

Infrared spectra suggest bond vibrations, mass spectra constrain mass and fragmentation, and NMR constrains local environments. No single functional-group band establishes the entire molecular structure. Astronomical detections additionally require line assignments, excitation models and confusion checks.


### Transfer derivation and failure case

For a neutral closed-shell formula with ordinary valences, the hydrogen-deficiency index is $\mathrm{DBE}=(2C+2+N-H-X)/2$, with $X$ the halogen count and oxygen omitted. Each ring or double bond contributes one and a triple bond contributes two. This formula constrains possible structures but does not identify connectivity or a functional group. Ethanol and dimethyl ether both have formula C₂H₆O; spectroscopy or chemical evidence must distinguish them. Charged species, radicals and unusual valence states require checking the counting assumptions before interpreting a fractional result.


### Derive, test and transfer

A local harmonic bond-stretch model has angular frequency sqrt(k/μ), with μ the reduced mass. A stronger effective restoring coefficient raises frequency, while heavier isotopes lower it. Functional groups can therefore have characteristic spectral regions, but the full environment and mode coupling shift them.

A structural assignment also needs atom connectivity, charge, stereochemistry and complementary evidence. A single matching frequency is not a unique molecular identification. The model isolates one bond coordinate, not an exact full molecular vibration. Electron counting and resonance structures are bookkeeping representations of a quantum state, not separate rapidly switching molecular species.
