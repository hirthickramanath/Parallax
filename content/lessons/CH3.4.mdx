<LessonSection id="story" title="Why the question matters" layer="story">

Prebiotic and astrobiological chemistry requires pathways, environmental plausibility and alternative explanations rather than semantic labels.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="intuition" title="Build a mental model" layer="intuition">

<LessonSection id="concept" title="Definitions and hypotheses" layer="concept">

Organic chemistry concerns carbon-containing molecular structures under the chosen scope; prebiotic relevance and biological origin are distinct hypotheses.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derivation, proof and limitations" layer="theory" fullOnly>

A reaction path must conserve atoms/charge and include kinetics, energy sources and environmental survival. Thermodynamic favourability alone does not prove a rapid pathway. For a simple barrier model $k\propto e^{-E_a/(k_BT)}$ is a qualified approximation with prefactors and tunnelling/photochemical limitations.

Photons, energetic particles, mineral surfaces and ice processing can synthesize complex species without life. Production of one precursor in a laboratory analogue does not establish a complete self-sustaining biological system or its extraterrestrial occurrence.

A claimed biosignature must be evaluated against abiotic sources, sinks, transport and measurement artifacts. Chirality or isotope fractionation may be informative but requires contamination and nonbiological alternative tests. A catalogue match remains a spectroscopic inference first.

Use precise evidence levels: species identification, environmental chemistry, plausible prebiotic role and biological interpretation. Preserve experimental/observational provenance and distinguish a hypothesis from a demonstrated mechanism. This supporting unit does not expand into an unrelated full biology curriculum.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="interactive" title="Investigate the claim" layer="interactive">

Create a claim-evidence ladder for a hypothetical organic detection. Identify at least one abiotic route, one kinetic bottleneck and one contamination test before discussing biological significance.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="worked" title="A worked argument" layer="worked" fullOnly>

In a stated Arrhenius ratio with equal prefactors and barrier difference k_BT ln10, the higher-barrier rate is one tenth. That relative-rate model alone says nothing about whether either pathway is biological.

</LessonSection>
