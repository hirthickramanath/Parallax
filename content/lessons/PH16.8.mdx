<LessonSection id="concept" title="Definitions and hypotheses" layer="concept">

Quantum-gravity research asks how spacetime/gravity and quantum principles fit beyond regimes where classical geometry plus ordinary quantum fields is adequate.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="theory" title="Derivation, proof and limitations" layer="theory" fullOnly>

The Planck length is $\ell_P=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}$ and Planck energy is √(ℏc⁵/G). These dimensional scales identify combinations of constants, not a proof that every experiment reaches new effects exactly at one universal length.

Quantum fields on curved backgrounds support controlled questions such as particle production or semiclassical stress under specified states/renormalization. Backreaction, singularities and full quantum geometry require additional structure. An information paradox is not resolved merely by naming a favourite microscopic model.

Effective quantum gravity can organize low-energy corrections where an expansion is valid. String, loop, asymptotic-safety, causal and other research directions make different assumptions and have different evidence/limits. A draft introduction should not present conjectural ultraviolet completion as established observation.

Evaluate claims through consistency, controlled limits, testable observables and honest uncertainty. Absence of a laboratory signal does not prove every speculative mechanism false, but mathematical elegance alone does not establish physical truth. Record dated primary sources and separate calculations reproduced from proposals merely read.

</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="worked" title="A worked argument" layer="worked" fullOnly>

In normalized units ℏ=4,G=9,c=1, the Planck-length combination is 6. If c doubles with those formal constants fixed, that length changes by 2⁻³ᐟ². This unit exercise is not a physical experiment varying fundamental constants.

</LessonSection>
